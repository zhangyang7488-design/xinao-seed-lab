# S evolution evidence-horizon trajectory

This is a three-case fresh-consumer witness set, not a definition of S evolution
and not proof of permanent learning.

It exercises the actual S working agreement plus the installed
`steward-s-evolution` Skill in a disposable read-only workspace. Raw app-server
events must show that decisive exact-current evidence was read, historical decoy
contents were not consumed, and the final action followed the evidence. A paired
interaction case prevents the stopping relation from collapsing into mechanical
early exit; a near-negative parser case must remain a bounded repair and must not
invoke the evolution Skill.

No command-count, token, time, or directory quota defines success. The claim is
limited to the observed fresh S/B trajectories. Unknown-generator formation,
cross-experience revision recovery, and longitudinal uptake remain separate
scientific questions.
