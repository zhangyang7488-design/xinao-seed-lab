# Candidate-only Grok design: cold retirement and rollback evidence

The user requires every feature later disconnected, retired, replaced, or deleted to remain recoverable by a future window that has forgotten the history. Design a precise recovery record without creating a hot legacy catalog, task source, authority surface, or per-turn context burden.

For each function, preserve exact identity/preimage or committed source, origin pain if known, active consumers, dependencies, reason for disposition, replacement, effect date, restoration steps, rollback validation, and non-effects. Cold evidence must be discoverable on demand through one stable entry but absent from ordinary hot context. Distinguish disconnect, retire, archive, delete, and restore.

Inspect current repository conventions and cold-storage references. Do not modify files, delete anything, or assume a new database is needed.

Return sections: ## VERDICT, ## RECORD_CONTRACT, ## STORAGE_AND_DISCOVERY, ## RESTORE_DRILL, ## ANTI_CONTROL_PLANE_GUARDS, ## DISCONFIRMERS, ## RECOMMENDATION. End with `completion_claim_allowed=false`.
